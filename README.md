### ALGO-000070: Does Frege polynomially simulate Extended Frege?
- **Subfield:** Proof complexity and metacomplexity
- **Open:** Determine whether extension variables can always be eliminated from Extended Frege proofs with only polynomial growth and polynomial-time translation.
- **Status:** Open for decades. This compares proof-system strength directly and is not identical to proving lower bounds for either system.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Determines whether introducing names for intermediate propositions creates a superpolynomial proof advantage.
- **Key works:** Cook and Reckhow (1979); Krajíček (2019), *Proof Complexity*; Pich–Santhanam’s Extended Frege work.
- **Related:** Programming languages—sharing and abstraction in proof terms.
